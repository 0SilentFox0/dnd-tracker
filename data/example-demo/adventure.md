# The Bell Beneath Briarwood

*A short original 5e adventure for four characters of 3rd level.*

## Hook

Each spring, three bronze notes drift out of Briarwood even though the old bell tower fell a century ago. This year the notes have stopped, the creek has turned silver, and a grove keeper named Mara Venn has vanished. The villagers ask the party to find her before the forest paths fold shut at dusk.

## Places and people

- **Alderwake** is a mill village built around a spring-fed wheel. Its bellmaker, Ivo Pell, recognizes the notes as a sequence used to tune a buried instrument.
- **Mara Venn**, a patient keeper of trails, followed silver moss toward the old listening grove. She is trapped but alive.
- **Tamsin Reed**, a young courier, saw lanterns move against the wind and can point the party toward the creek crossing.
- **The Briar Warden** is a stone-and-root guardian built to protect the grove's listening chamber. A damaged tuning fork has made it mistake every visitor for a threat.

## Scene flow

1. In Alderwake, question Ivo and Tamsin. Their clues reveal the order: spring, branch, stone.
2. At the listening grove, solve the three-tone gate. A wrong tone causes harmless thorn growth and gives the party a new clue from Mara's trail marks.
3. In the root vault, navigate a small shifting maze and reach the Warden's dais.
4. The Warden's fork can be silenced with the correct sequence or repaired with Ivo's tuning pin. The party can also defeat the guardian, then free Mara and carry the fork home.

## Puzzle: the three listening bowls

Three stone bowls bear a droplet, a leaf, and a mountain. The gate opens when they are sounded in the order that water takes through the wood: **droplet, leaf, mountain**. Ivo can tell the party that the old bellmaker's rhyme names spring before branch and branch before stone. There is no time limit; a failed attempt produces a soft chime and a visible hint rather than damage.

## GM notes

Keep the Warden protective rather than malicious. It powers down if a character uses the tuning pin and speaks Mara's name. Reward careful listening, patient negotiation, and creative use of the map. The maze is a navigation scene, not a punishment: each wrong turn returns the group to the central chamber with a new echo clue.
