## Suggested task groups

1. **Battle load egress (A01, A03).** Make the access recheck membership-only, deduplicate it across the default and scoped deps, and load campaign rules only for PREPARED battles with a memoized `parseRules`. One implementer; check with pipeline tests and battle-sweep.
2. **Pipeline layering and guards (A05, A06, A07, A18).** Move the record op, `executeBattleOperation` and deps to `lib/server/battles`. Add the core/HTTP split for `runGuardedBattleOperation`, shared execution/DM guards, the API_ERRORS strings, and a single victory/dryRun evaluation.
3. **Save DC and dice math (A02, A11, A21, A22).** Route every DC through `getSpellSaveDC`/`monsterSaveDc` with `rules.formulas.saveDcBase`, and add the recharge and ability-label constants.
4. **Roll modifier passes (A04, A26).** Use a single `collectModifiers` flags pass for saves and checks, and hoist per-enemy data in opportunity attacks.
5. **Item stats (A09, A10, A17).** Share the bounds constants between `readStats` and zod, add a WeakMap cache, and add the spell-grant field and pool-key helpers.
6. **Choices traversal (A12, A27 selections part, A28).** Add the `forEachChoiceOwner` walker for selections, prune and visitPicks, and reuse resolved class entries.
7. **Participant builders and turn decomposition (A13, A14, A24).** Extract helpers from the character/unit builders, the turn loop and turn start, and drop the dead per-loader DB fallbacks.
8. **Constants hygiene (A15, A16, A19, A20, A23).** `EffectMarker`, condition predicate dedupe, `RULES_EDITIONS`, moving SRD tables to lib/constants, and one dice-text module.
9. **Import-layer cleanup (A08, A25, A27 table/stash parts, A29).** Move the compendium limits out of lib/server, batch the persist updates, and split the stash handlers.
